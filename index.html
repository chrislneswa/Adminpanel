How to use:

2. Replace the placeholder target with your real hosting IP / hostname.
3. Open Cloudflare → your domain → DNS → Records → Add record and enter each row exactly as shown.
4. Leave Proxy status on Proxied (orange cloud) unless your host tells you otherwise.
